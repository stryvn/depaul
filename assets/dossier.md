## 2\. Institutional Vision, Mission & Philosophy

### Vision

> *"To empower every learner with knowledge, wisdom, and values, enabling them to become compassionate, innovative, and responsible global citizens who contribute positively to society."*

### Mission

* **Vincentian Tradition**: Providing quality, value-based education rooted in the spiritual tradition and compassionate service of St. Vincent de Paul.  
* **Academic Rigour**: Encouraging academic excellence through innovative teaching methods and continuous holistic evaluation.  
* **Integrity & Discipline**: Promoting mutual respect, moral integrity, self-discipline, and character formation.  
* **Future-Ready Skills**: Fostering creativity, critical thinking, leadership, coding, robotics, and lifelong learning.  
* **Inclusive Environment**: Creating a safe, nurturing, CCTV-secured, and student-centred learning ecosystem.  
* **Service to Society**: Inspiring students to serve their community with empathy, active civic responsibility, and compassion.
